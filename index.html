How publicly funded long-term care works in BC

Families don't apply to a subsidized long-term care home directly. An Island Health case manager assesses the person's needs. The family can list preferred homes, but the person must usually accept the first appropriate bed that comes up. They can ask to transfer to a preferred home later. Private-pay homes and home-care companies take enquiries directly.

Start the process through Island Health Home & Community Care, or ask your family doctor or hospital social worker for a referral.
